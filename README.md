# Disaster-Management-
A comprehensive project focused on Disaster Management, covering disaster preparedness, risk mitigation strategies, emergency planning, response during disasters, and post-disaster recovery. The repository aims to provide resources, tools, and insights to improve resilience and ensure effective management before, during, and after disaster events.


##  Project Overview
Disaster Management is the process of preparing for, responding to, and recovering from disasters to minimize their impact on human life, property, and the environment. This project focuses on the strategies and practices involved in managing disasters effectively before, during, and after their occurrence.

---

##  Objectives
- Understand the fundamentals of disaster management.
- Identify various natural and human-induced disasters.
- Study disaster risk reduction and mitigation techniques.
- Explore preparedness and emergency planning methods.
- Understand disaster response procedures.
- Learn recovery and rehabilitation practices.

##  Phases of Disaster Management

### 1. Before a Disaster (Mitigation & Preparedness)
- Hazard identification and risk assessment.
- Disaster risk reduction strategies.
- Community awareness and education programs.
- Emergency planning and resource allocation.
- Training programs and mock drills.

### 2. During a Disaster (Response)
- Activation of emergency response plans.
- Search and rescue operations.
- Evacuation and shelter management.
- Medical aid and relief distribution.
- Communication and coordination among agencies.

### 3. After a Disaster (Recovery & Rehabilitation)
- Damage assessment and reporting.
- Restoration of essential services.
- Rehabilitation of affected communities.
- Reconstruction of infrastructure.
- Review of lessons learned for future preparedness.
